\section{Hand Analysis (1.3)}

\subsection{FO4 delay of this library}

FO4 = delay of an inverter driving four copies of itself. Using \texttt{INVX8}
($C_{in} = 2.892$\,fF, load $4C_{in} = 11.57$\,fF, input slew 30\,ps) in the slow library:
rise 39.2\,ps, fall 46.2\,ps, so
\[
t_{FO4} = (39.2 + 46.2)/2 \approx 43\ \text{ps}.
\]
(\texttt{INVX1} is not used: $4C_{in}$ falls below the smallest load in its table.)
\todo{read $C_{in}$ and the rise/fall tables in the .lib and confirm these values}

For scale: clk$\rightarrow$Q (271\,ps) $\approx$ 6 FO4, setup (215\,ps) $\approx$ 5 FO4,
one full adder \texttt{ADDFHX} on the critical path (350--380\,ps) $\approx$ 8--9 FO4.

Genus (1.2a): combinational delay $= 4935 - 271 = 4664$\,ps $= 4664/43 \approx \mathbf{1.1\times10^2}$\,FO4,
close to the hand estimate (about 100 FO4, 4.3\,ns).
Genus already does better than the RTL as written: the seven chained additions become one
carry-save tree (\texttt{csa\_tree\_ADD\_TC\_OP}). It is slower than the estimate mainly because of
effects the estimate ignores: fanout buffers (173\,ps) and the mask AND (130\,ps) on the path.

\subsection{Shortest period with the existing registers (1.3b)}

If the three \texttt{sum\_out\_d0\_d*} stages were moved into the multiply-add (retiming) and split
the logic evenly:
\[
T_{min} \approx \frac{4664}{3} + \underbrace{271}_{\text{clk}\rightarrow\text{Q}} + \underbrace{215}_{\text{setup}} + \underbrace{50}_{\text{uncert.}}
\approx 1555 + 536 \approx \mathbf{2.1\ ns}.
\]

What limits it:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Register overhead is fixed.} Each stage pays 536\,ps ($\approx$12 FO4) regardless of the logic.
  \item \textbf{Cells cannot be split.} A full adder is $\approx$370\,ps, so cuts cannot land exactly at 1/3.
  \item \textbf{Other paths take over.} Enable/control, weight-load and I/O paths become critical.
  \item \textbf{Wider registers.} Cutting inside the arithmetic stores 8$\times$16-bit products instead of one
        19-bit sum, adding area and power.
\end{itemize}
